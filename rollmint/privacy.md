# Rollmint Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

Rollmint is an iPhone app by Josh Manson ("we") that helps you review and delete photos and videos from your photo library. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Summary
- **Your photos never leave your phone.** Rollmint reads and deletes photos only on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and photos are not sent to us or to anyone else.
- **We do not collect any data.** Rollmint has no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking, and we run no servers that receive your data.
- **Purchases** are processed by Apple. RevenueCat records purchase history so your purchase can be unlocked and restored.

## Photos and videos
Rollmint asks for access to your photo library so it can show you your photos and videos and delete the ones you pick. All of this happens on your device: browsing, grouping by month, filtering screenshots, and estimating storage. Deletions go through Apple's Photos framework, and iOS asks you to confirm each one. Deleted items move to the Photos "Recently Deleted" album, where iOS keeps them for up to 30 days. Your choices and deletion counts are stored only on your device.

## Month Picks and recap cards
If you mark photos as "picks", Rollmint can add them to an album named "Best of <Month Year>" in your own photo library. It can also make a recap image (a 3×3 grid of your picks with simple stats). The recap is created on your device. It is shared only if you choose to share it yourself through the iOS share sheet, and Rollmint never sends it anywhere.

## Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple)
Rollmint offers a one-time "Pro" purchase.
- **Apple** handles all payments through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.
- **RevenueCat, Inc.** is the service we use to check and restore purchases. When you buy or restore, RevenueCat processes **purchase history**: the App Store transaction and receipt details, together with a random anonymous app user ID that RevenueCat generates. This data is **not linked to your identity**. It is used only to provide app functionality (unlocking Pro and restoring it) and is **not used for tracking** or advertising. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>.

## Tracking and advertising
Rollmint does not track you across apps or websites, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Children
Rollmint is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Your choices
You can change or revoke photo access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Rollmint. Deleting the app removes its on-device data. One exception: the free-deletion counter is kept in the device Keychain so it survives a reinstall, and it holds no personal information.
