# Rollmint Support

Need help with Rollmint? Email **[RollmintSupport@icloud.com](mailto:RollmintSupport@icloud.com)**. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the app version (shown in Rollmint › Settings).

## FAQ

**Why didn't my storage go up right away after deleting?**
Deleted photos and videos move to the Photos **Recently Deleted** album and keep using space until that album is emptied, or until iOS removes them after 30 days. To free the space now, open Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted › Select › Delete All.

**Can I get a photo back?**
Yes. Within 30 days you can recover it from Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted.

**Rollmint only shows some of my photos.**
You may have given limited access. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Rollmint and choose **Full Access**, or add more photos to your selection.

**How do I restore my Pro purchase?**
Open Rollmint › Settings › **Restore Purchases** while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I get a refund?**
Apple handles refunds. Visit <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>.

**Does Rollmint upload my photos?**
No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
